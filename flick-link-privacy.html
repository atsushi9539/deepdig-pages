Privacy Policy — Flick Link

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Flick Link ("the App", "the Game"), developed by AshDevjp ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. By playing the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Store Locally

Your game progress — cleared stages, stars, lives, hint points and settings such as sound — is saved on your own device, using standard local storage. Apart from the star total described in section 2 (only if you open the Star Ranking), this data is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes this progress.

We do not require or offer account creation, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

2. Star Ranking

The App has an optional Star Ranking. Nothing is sent until you open the ranking screen for the first time. After that, the App sends the following to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) when you open the ranking or when your star total increases:

  • a random code created on your device for the ranking (it is not linked to your Apple Account, advertising identifier or device identifiers; the server stores only a one-way hash of it), and
  • the total number of stars you have earned, and
  • a nickname, only if you choose to set one.

By default, your name in the ranking (an animal and a four-digit number) is generated automatically from that code. You may instead set a nickname of up to 12 characters, which is shown to other players together with a four-digit number. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname. Nicknames are checked by an automatic filter; other players can report a nickname or hide a player from their own ranking. A nickname reported by several players is reset to the automatic name, and we keep the reported nickname with the report for review. You can return to the automatic name at any time. We do not collect your real name or contact details. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request; we do not store it. We use this data only to show the ranking and to remove obviously invalid scores.

Ranking data (including any nickname and reports) is kept while the ranking is offered. Uninstalling the App deletes the code from your device, after which your entry can no longer be linked to you. To have your entry deleted, contact us (see Contact) and include the name shown for you in the ranking.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads served through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between stages, and optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for a life or hint points). To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, language, approximate region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

On iOS, the App will ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown — they will simply be less personalized.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-App Purchase

The App offers an optional one-time purchase (the "Unlimited Pass"). Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. The App only checks with the App Store whether the pass has been purchased on your Apple Account, so that it can be restored on reinstall or on another device. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

5. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized or data is used for advertising purposes, as required by the GDPR and related laws. You can review or change your choice at any time from the stage list in the App (the "Ad privacy settings" link below the pass).

The legal basis for advertising-related processing is your consent (or, where permitted, Google's legitimate interest). You have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data held by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for how to exercise these rights.

6. Children's Privacy

Flick Link is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Aside from the advertising data described above (handled directly between your device and Google), purchase verification handled by Apple, and Cloudflare acting as the hosting provider for the Star Ranking, we do not share any data with third parties.

8. Your Choices

  • You can decline App Tracking Transparency at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your ad consent in the App via the "Ad privacy settings" link in the stage list.
  • You can reset your ad identifier or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.
  • If you never open the Star Ranking, no ranking data is sent.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information listed on our X (Twitter) profile: @AshDevjp.
