Privacy Policy — One Campfire (ひとつの焚き火)

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data One Campfire ("the App", "the Game"), developed by AshDevjp ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. By playing the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Store Locally

Your game progress — resources, villages, villager levels, story progress, and settings such as language and volume — is saved only on your own device, using standard local storage. This data is never transmitted to us and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes this progress.

We do not require or offer account creation, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

2. Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus) served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, language, approximate region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

On iOS, the App will ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you can still watch rewarded ads — they will simply be less personalized.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized or data is used for advertising purposes, as required by the GDPR and related laws. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the App.

The legal basis for advertising-related processing is your consent (or, where permitted, Google's legitimate interest). You have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal data held by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for how to exercise these rights.

4. Children's Privacy

One Campfire is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Aside from the advertising data described above (handled directly between your device and Google), we do not share any data with third parties.

6. Your Choices

  • You can decline App Tracking Transparency at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your ad consent in the App via Settings → Ad privacy.
  • You can reset your ad identifier or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information listed on our X (Twitter) profile: @AshDevjp.
