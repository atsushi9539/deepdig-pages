Frequently Asked Questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete your save data, so please be careful.

Do I need to pay to play?
No. Deep Dig is free to play. Optional rewarded video ads can be watched for in-game bonuses like stamina refills, but they are never required.

The game froze or crashed — what should I do?
Please force-close the app and reopen it. If the problem keeps happening, let us know via the contact link above — details like your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing right before it happened, help us fix it faster.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Please reach out through the contact link above. We read every message.
