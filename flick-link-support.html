Frequently Asked Questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Cleared stages, stars, lives and hint points are saved automatically on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this progress, so please be careful.

I bought the Unlimited Pass. How do I get it back on a new device or after reinstalling?

Open the stage list and tap "Restore purchases". The pass is linked to your Apple Account, so it comes back as long as you are signed in with the same account. The pass is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

What does the Unlimited Pass include?

Unlimited lives, hints and undos, and no ads between stages. The small banner at the bottom of the screen remains.

I ran out of lives. What now?

Lives refill by one every 5 minutes. You can also watch an optional video ad to get one back right away.

I'm stuck on a stage.

Use a hint to see the next move. If you have reached a dead end, the hint tells you how many moves to go back — this costs no points. Every stage has been checked by computer to be solvable.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language. You can also change it with the 🌐 menu at the bottom of the stage list.

How does the Star Ranking work? Can I change my name?

Open "🏆 Star ranking" at the top of the stage list, or "🏆 Ranking" on the title screen. Your name starts as an animal and a four-digit number. Tap "Change name" to set a nickname of up to 12 characters — the four-digit number is always shown with it. Please don't use your real name or other personal information. Offensive names are blocked by a filter, and "Use the automatic name" switches back at any time.

I saw an offensive name in the ranking.

Tap "⋯" next to that player and choose "Report name". A name reported by several players is automatically reset to the automatic name. Choose "Block" to hide that player from your own ranking ("Unblock all" brings everyone back). You can also report a name to us directly via the contact link above, and we will remove names that break these rules.

How do I delete my ranking entry?

Send us a DM with the name shown for you in the ranking, including the four-digit number, and we will delete it. If you uninstall the app, your old entry can no longer be linked to you.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, tap "Ad privacy settings" in the stage list. On iOS you can also turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The game froze or crashed — what should I do?

Please force-close the app and reopen it. If the problem keeps happening, let us know via the contact link above. Your device model, iOS version and the stage number help us fix it faster.
